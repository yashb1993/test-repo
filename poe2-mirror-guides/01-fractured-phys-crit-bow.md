# Guide 1: Fractured Physical Crit Bow

**For:** Deadeye / Pathfinder physical or conversion bow builds (Lightning Arrow, etc.).
**Difficulty:** Medium. **Rough budget:** 150–400+ div. **Patch basis:** 0.5.x

## Target item

| Side | Mods (all T1 or T2 at minimum) |
|------|--------------------------------|
| Prefix 1 | **% increased Physical Damage** |
| Prefix 2 | **Adds # to # Physical Damage** |
| Prefix 3 | Hybrid % Phys / Accuracy, or 3rd damage prefix your build wants |
| Suffix 1 | **+% Critical Hit Chance** (aim for the 5% top roll) |
| Suffix 2 | **+# to Level of all Projectile Skills** or **+1/+2 Arrows** (build-dependent) |
| Suffix 3 | Attack Speed or Crit Damage Bonus |

**Base:** Spine Bow or Thicket Bow, **ilvl 82+ (85+ preferred)**.

## Phase 1: Buy the fractured base (don't roll it)

1. On trade, search for a **rare Spine/Thicket Bow, ilvl 82+**, with **one** of these **fractured** at T1:
   - `% increased Physical Damage` (best, hardest to hit later)
   - `Adds # to # Physical Damage`
   - `+% Critical Hit Chance`
2. If the rest of the bow is junk, that's fine; it'll be cheaper.
3. **Shortcut:** a bow already carrying fractured crit **plus** one good prefix is often worth the extra price.

> Why fractured: a Fracturing Orb locks a *random* mod and needs ≥4 mods. Fracturing your own bow wastes many orbs. Let someone else pay for that randomness.

## Phase 2: Clean the item

Goal: keep only the fractured mod (plus any T1 mod that's on the target list).

1. Remove unwanted prefixes with **Orb of Annulment + Omen of Sinistral Annulment**.
2. Remove unwanted suffixes with **Orb of Annulment + Omen of Dextral Annulment**.
3. The fractured mod can't be annulled, so it's safe throughout.

**Bail-out:** if an annul removes a good mod, either re-add it (Phase 3) or sell the bow back as a "fractured base + 1".

## Phase 3: Prefixes

1. **Perfect Exalted Orb + Omen of Sinistral Exaltation.** Only adds a prefix.
2. If you have **two** open prefixes, consider **Omen of Greater Exaltation** stacked with Sinistral to add two prefixes at once (check that the omens stack in your patch).
3. Bad prefix → **Chaos Orb + Omen of Sinistral Erasure** to reroll a prefix only. Or use **Whittling** if the bad mod has the lowest required ilvl.
4. Optional shortcut: **Greater Essence of Abrasion** adds guaranteed flat phys. **Remember: only one crafted mod per item in 0.5.** If you use it here, you can't use an essence later.

**Sellable checkpoint:** fractured T1 + two T1 phys prefixes is a high-value bow on its own. If you're running low on currency, **sell here**. That's a strong partial result.

## Phase 4: Suffixes

1. **Perfect Exalted Orb + Omen of Dextral Exaltation** for each open suffix.
2. Projectile/arrow suffixes have low weight. **Desecration** is the better tool for the last suffix:
   - Use a weapon bone + **Omen of Dextral Necromancy** → suffix desecration.
   - Choose from 3 options. If none fit, use **Omen of Abyssal Echoes** to reroll the options.
   - If the result is bad, **Omen of Light** removes only the desecrated mod, so you can try again.
3. Bad suffix → **Chaos + Dextral Erasure**.

## Phase 5: Divine and finish

1. **Divine Orb** until every mod is near the top of its range. In the past this has often been **the most expensive step**.
2. Decide on a stopping point in advance, for example "within 90% of max on phys% and flat phys". Chasing perfect rolls can double your cost.
3. List the bow. If it's genuinely top 1%, it'll get **mirror service** offers, which earn currency for craft #2.

## Common mistakes

- Using **Whittling** expecting it to hit the *lowest tier*. In 0.5 it hits the lowest **required item level**.
- Using an essence early, then wanting a different crafted mod later. You only get **one**.
- Buying a sub-ilvl-82 base. You can't roll top-tier mods on it.
