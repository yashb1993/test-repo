# Guide 2: Caster Wand / Staff

**For:** spell builds (Sorceress, Stormweaver, Chronomancer, Lich, etc.).
**Difficulty:** Hard. **Rough budget:** 200–500+ div. **Patch basis:** 0.5.x (older guides adapted)

## Target item

| Side | Mods |
|------|------|
| Prefix 1 | **T1 % increased Spell Damage** |
| Prefix 2 | **+# to Level of all [your element] Spell Skills** |
| Prefix 3 | **Gain % of Damage as extra [your element]**, or a 2nd spell-damage-type prefix |
| Suffix 1 | **% increased Cast Speed** (T1 ideal, **T2 is usually the smart choice**) |
| Suffix 2 | **+% Critical Hit Chance for Spells** |
| Suffix 3 | Crit Damage Bonus for Spells, or +Spirit/Mana (build-dependent) |

**Base:** a wand (or staff for 2-hand) at **ilvl 81+**. Older guides say ilvl 81 is enough for the key wand mods; 85+ is safer.

## Phase 1: Secure T1 Spell Damage (the expensive gate)

Older guides report **~200–300 Greater Chaos Orbs** on average to isolate T1 Spell Damage, and up to **~200 div** for the equivalent staff step. Avoid paying that yourself:

1. **Buy** a wand with **fractured T1 % Spell Damage** (best), or a magic wand with T1 Spell Damage.
2. If you can't find one: buy several cheap ilvl 81+ wands with T1 Spell Damage + junk, and use Phase 2 to clean them.

**Sellable checkpoint:** fractured T1 Spell Damage on a clean base sells well. You can flip extras to fund the rest.

## Phase 2: Clean and choose your element

1. Remove everything except T1 Spell Damage:
   - **Annulment + Sinistral/Dextral Annulment** for a specific side.
   - **Chaos + Whittling** when the junk mod has the lowest required ilvl.
2. **Decide your element now** (Lightning/Cold/Fire/Chaos). Every later prefix must match it.

## Phase 3: Prefixes 2 and 3

1. **Perfect Exalted Orb + Omen of Sinistral Exaltation.**
2. Wrong element (e.g. "Gain as Fire" on a Lightning build) → **Chaos + Sinistral Erasure** and try again.
3. If one specific prefix keeps missing, check whether a **Perfect Essence** guarantees it. You only get **one crafted mod**, so save it for the hardest-to-hit mod.

**Sellable checkpoint:** 3 matching T1/T2 prefixes, open suffixes. This sells well to people who want to finish suffixes themselves.

## Phase 4: Suffixes

1. **Perfect Exalted + Omen of Dextral Exaltation**, targeting Cast Speed and Spell Crit.
2. **Accept T2 Cast Speed.** Older guides agree it's usually the better use of currency when prefixes aren't perfect.
3. Last suffix: **Desecration + Omen of Dextral Necromancy**, then **Abyssal Echoes** to reroll the options and **Omen of Light** to remove a bad result.

## Phase 5: Divine and finish

- Divine with priority: Spell Damage% > +Levels (fixed) > Gain as Extra > Cast Speed.
- Set a divine cap before starting, e.g. 30–60 div. Stop once you hit it.

## Alternative 0.5 route: staff via fractured +Levels

A 0.5.0 staff guide ([PoE Currency](https://www.poecurrency.com/news/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-how-to-craft-perfect-endgame-staff-for-50-divine-orbs), which claims about 50 div) and the [+10 Level Staff video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VLc88wYNgfw) lock **+Levels to Spell Skills** instead of Spell Damage:

1. Get **ilvl 81+** staves with **+5 to Level of all Spell Skills**. Divine them up to **+6**.
2. Prepare **~5 of these bases**. **Fracturing Orb** each one until the +6 Levels mod is the one that gets fractured.
3. Remove the other mods. Then **Chaos** (with Dextral Erasure) for **Spell Crit Chance**.
4. Finish suffixes: Cast Speed, plus your element's damage/levels.
5. Watch for **Mana** mods. A wand guide warns they have very high roll weight and can ruin later steps, so block or remove them early.

Other 0.5 estimates put a flawless staff at **50–100 div**, much cheaper than older wand estimates. Treat both numbers as rough.

## Notes for staff

- Same process, but staff prefixes give larger values and more open slots for the price. A mirror staff is often cheaper per point of DPS than wand + focus.
- Older staff guides started from an **ilvl 80+ staff with 3 sockets**. Check how sockets/runes work in your patch (0.5.5 added Runeforging).
