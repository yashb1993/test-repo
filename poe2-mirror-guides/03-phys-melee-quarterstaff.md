# Guide 3: Physical Quarterstaff / Spear

**For:** Monk / Invoker quarterstaff builds, or Huntress spear builds.
**Difficulty:** Medium. **Rough budget:** 150–400+ div. **Patch basis:** 0.5.x (bulk method adapted from 0.3 guides)

## Target item

| Side | Mods |
|------|------|
| Prefix 1 | **Adds # to # Physical Damage** (T1) |
| Prefix 2 | **% increased Physical Damage** (T1) |
| Prefix 3 | **Hybrid % Physical Damage + Accuracy** |
| Suffix 1 | **+% Critical Hit Chance** |
| Suffix 2 | **+% Critical Damage Bonus** |
| Suffix 3 | **% increased Attack Speed** |

Community guides consistently say the strongest versions run **three physical prefixes** with **crit chance + crit damage** as the top suffixes.

**Base:** highest-damage quarterstaff (or spear) base available, **ilvl 82+ (85+ preferred)**.

## Phase 1: Bulk-buy magic bases (the volume method)

This adapts the 0.3 "bulk crafting" method:

1. Buy **10–20 magic bases** (ilvl 75+ works for the bulk step, 82+ for the final item). Each must have:
   - a **high-tier flat Physical Damage prefix**, **and**
   - a **high-tier Attack Speed or Crit suffix**.
2. Magic items with 2 good mods are cheap because people don't value them, which makes this method cost-effective.

## Phase 2: Regal and slam prefixes

1. **Regal Orb** (optionally with **Omen of Dextral Coronation** if you want the 3rd mod to be a suffix).
   - Note: **Sinistral Coronation may no longer exist** in 0.5. Don't plan around it.
2. **Greater/Perfect Exalted Orb + Omen of Greater Exaltation** (+ Sinistral if it stacks) to add 2 prefixes at once.
3. Sort your results:
   - **3 good phys prefixes** → continue to Phase 3.
   - **2 good + 1 bad** → **Chaos + Sinistral Erasure**, or sell as a mid-tier weapon.
   - **Bricked** → sell to recover currency. Don't sink more into it.

**Sellable checkpoint:** 3 T1 phys prefixes + 1 good suffix is a huge DPS weapon. Even unfinished, quarterstaff/spear phys bases sell quickly. Use them to fund the next attempt.

## Phase 3: Fracture (optional, when you have a keeper)

If you have a 4+ mod item with a T1 mod you'd hate to lose:
- A **Fracturing Orb** locks **one random** mod. It's only worth it if most of the mods are ones you'd be happy to have locked.
- Otherwise skip this and protect mods with Sinistral/Dextral omens instead.

## Phase 4: Suffixes

1. **Perfect Exalted + Omen of Dextral Exaltation** for open suffixes.
2. Last suffix: **Desecration + Dextral Necromancy**. Reroll options with **Abyssal Echoes**, remove a bad result with **Omen of Light**.
3. Or use your **one crafted mod** (essence) for crit or attack speed if one is available. It's often cheaper than repeated desecration.

## Phase 5: Divine and finish

- Divine priority: flat phys > % phys > hybrid > crit damage > crit chance > attack speed.
- Set your divine cap first.

## Why this is the best first craft for two mirrors

- Lots of attempts at low cost per attempt, and the leftovers have **real resale value**.
- Run 3–5 attempts in parallel. Finish the best one, sell the rest, and use the money for weapon #2 (or for Guide 1).
